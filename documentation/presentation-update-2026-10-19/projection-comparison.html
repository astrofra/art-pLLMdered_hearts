How to read the diagnostic numbers

5-neighbour overlap: the fraction of original nearest neighbours retained by each map, averaged over all 80 points. Nearest clip retained: among 55 comments, how often the visually nearest video point is the highest-scoring transcript in 768D. Neither metric rates artistic relevance. Recency and playback gates are excluded from these two diagnostics.

Parameter controls expose sensitivity. Defaults were selected before viewing the layouts: t-SNE perplexity 15; UMAP 15 neighbours, min_dist 0.1; seed 42. Smaller scales emphasise smaller neighbourhoods. The 13 recorded fits cover three scales and two seeds for each non-linear method, plus PCA.
